# Sky Verse Models

Download the required models and place them in `/sdcard/SkyVerse/models/` on your Android device.

## LLM Models (Choose one based on your phone RAM)
- **4GB RAM:** SmolLM2-135M-Instruct-Q4_K_M (80 MB)
- **6-8GB RAM:** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct-Q4_K_M (400 MB)
- **8+GB RAM:** Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-Q4_K_M (1.0 GB)
- **12+GB RAM:** Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct-Q4_K_M (1.9 GB)

## Required Voice Models
- `ggml-tiny.en.bin` (Whisper STT) - ~40 MB
- `ecapa_tdnn_int8.onnx` (Speaker ID) - ~12 MB
- OpenWakeWord ONNX files - ~2 MB
- Silero VAD ONNX - ~2 MB
